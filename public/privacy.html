Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

NO ZZZ is designed to minimize data collection and keep founders in control. This policy explains the data processed by the service.

Data we process

We process account details you provide, including your name, email address, salted password verifier, venture briefs, product interactions, and basic security logs such as request time, status, and IP address.

How data is used

Data is used to provide and secure the service, save your ventures, generate requested business analysis, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information.

AI processing

When an AI provider is configured, venture prompts may be sent to that provider to generate requested output. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, health records, or other unnecessary sensitive information. Provider processing is governed by its applicable terms.

Storage and security

Venture briefs are encrypted at rest. Passwords are protected with salted scrypt derivation. Sessions use secure browser cookies and server-side controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and choices

We retain account and venture data while the account is active or as needed for security and legal obligations. You may request access, correction, export, or deletion through the service operator.

Contact

Contact the service operator listed on the deployment’s official support channel for privacy requests. Identity verification may be required.
